DadStrong Privacy Policy

Effective Oct 28, 2026.

DadStrong is a workout log for busy fathers. Workout data stays on your device (and in backups you export yourself). We do not run accounts or ads.

If you enable Apple Health, finished workouts you choose to save are written to Apple Health on your iPhone. Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

Optional DadStrong Pro purchases are processed by Apple. Purchase status may be checked via RevenueCat solely to unlock Pro features.

Contact: open an issue on the project repository.
